Every swatch card is painted by its own custom property and shows the token name, its HEX and OKLCH
values; the card's copy button copies the HEX value. Primitive shades come from `colors.css`;
semantic roles and contrast pairings come from `index.css` and `utilities.css`.

<ColorsShowcase />

## Reading the palette

Primitive families present all declared numeric shades plus the family aliases such as `--paper`
and `--lake-forest-muted` as color-swatch cards. Semantic roles are shown with sample text on their
own background token, where a matching `*-foreground` token exists. Contrast pairings use the pairs
already defined by the theme rather than newly chosen color combinations.

The control section uses the real shared `Button` with documented props. Hover, focus and active
colors come from the same `control-color-*` utilities; the loading and disabled examples use the
component's own states.

## Responsive notes

Swatch-card grids collapse from four columns to one on narrow screens. The semantic cards and
pairing samples wrap to one column, so no horizontal scrolling is needed to read a token name or value.
